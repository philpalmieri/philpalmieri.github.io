**Marketplace:** https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/taskgregator

**Taskgregator** is an Obsidian plugin that gives you a Things-style task rollup while keeping tasks native to your notes.

Why I built it: I wanted one focused view of open work, without moving tasks into another app or losing note context.

## What it does

- Aggregates open tasks across your vault
- Keeps tasks in their original notes
- Helps surface stale/deferred work
- Stays markdown-first and Obsidian-native

## Install

1. Open Obsidian Community Plugins
2. Search for `Taskgregator`
3. Install and enable
