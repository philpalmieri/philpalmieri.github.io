**Taskgregator is now live in the Obsidian Community Plugins marketplace:**  
https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/taskgregator

I built **Taskgregator** because I wanted the best part of dedicated task apps (a clean, focused view of what matters now) without moving my tasks out of Obsidian.

I already capture work in notes where context lives: dailies, people notes, project pages, meeting notes. I did not want another inbox, another sync layer, or another place where tasks go to die. I wanted native Obsidian tasks, with better aggregation and better signal.

Taskgregator gives me that:

- A Things-style rollup of open tasks while tasks stay in their source notes
- Better visibility on what is stale, active, or repeatedly deferred
- A lightweight way to review priorities without breaking note context
- Native markdown workflow, no external task silo

If you run your life in Obsidian and want less task sprawl, this is for you.

Get it from the marketplace (recommended):  
https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/taskgregator

Code is here if you want to peek or contribute:  
https://github.com/philpalmieri/obsidian-taskgregator
